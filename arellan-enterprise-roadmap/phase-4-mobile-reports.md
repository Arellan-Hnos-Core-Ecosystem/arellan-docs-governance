# Phase 4: Mobile Executive Overviews & Dashboards (Months 9 - 10)

## 1. Operational Scope
Provide simplified, high-visibility mobile touchpoints for senior non-technical owners to execute continuous off-site operational audits.

## 2. Milestones and Deliverables
- **M4.1: Flutter Executive Hub:** Native iOS/Android compilation of the owner oversight platform.
- **M4.2: Live CCTV Cloud Stream:** Ingestion of workshop IP cameras into the mobile dashboard interface.
- **M4.3: Real-Time Anomaly Flagging:** Instant push notifications regarding unauthorized geolocation movements or sudden cashbox overruns.
