# Strategic Roadmap Overview: 2026 - 2028

## 1. Executive Summary
This roadmap establishes the phased professionalization of the automotive clinic, migrating from error-prone manual operations to a secure, decentralized digital ecosystem. The layout balances short-term security remediation (stopping asset and cash flight) with long-term commercialization goals (SaaS boilerplate extraction).

## 2. Global Strategy Matrix
- **Year 1 (2026):** Leakage control, biometric tracking, inventory audits, and transactional core deployment.
- **Year 2 (2027):** Mobilization, executive intelligence dashboards, customer portals, and vendor automation.
- **Year 3 (2028):** Multi-tenant partitioning, predictive AI routines, and market expansion.
