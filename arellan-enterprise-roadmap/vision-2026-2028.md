# Enterprise Architectural Vision: 2026 - 2028

## 1. The Ultimate Goal
To transform traditional, highly insecure automotive service operations into data-driven, auditable, and self-optimizing digital environments. 

## 2. Architectural Pillars
- **Zero Trust Internally:** Verification over trust. Every manual touchpoint is recorded.
- **Absolute Modular Decoupling:** Complete multi-repo isolation to ensure seamless software maintenance.
- **SaaS First Mentality:** Brand-agnostic design definitions across all 13 development nodes.
